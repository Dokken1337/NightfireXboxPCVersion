# Changelog

## v1.6 (2026-11-21)
Ported from TGP482's NightFire port (MIT licence), and added to PC Settings and the F1 window:
- Uncapped frame rate: the game steps by real time, so it keeps its normal speed at 120, 144, 240 fps or more. The
  things that counted 60 fps frames (gadget recharge, grapple, helicopters, particles, glass, debris, doors, timers,
  shots, menu key repeat) keep their pace. Frame Cap now offers 30 to 360 or Unlimited. The driving missions stay at
  the console's rate.
- Ambient occlusion: SSAO or GTAO, fading out with the game's fog.
- HDR10 output on a display with Windows HDR on.
- 5.1 surround sound (automatic on a surround device), stereo or mono.
- Texture packs (`textures\replacement`) and texture dumping (`textures\dumps`, PNG or DDS).
- Gyro aiming (DualSense, DualShock 4, Switch Pro and others): on foot, in zero gravity and on mounted guns, with
  sensitivity and axis settings.
- PC Settings is now a scrolling list with the new rows.

## v1.5 (2026-11-21)
- Driving missions: the hint pictures show PC keys and mouse buttons (for example "Pull [left mouse button] to
  Fire" in Paris Prelude) instead of the Xbox controller's.
- Equinox: firing the Phoenix Samurai no longer pushes Bond down through the floor.
- Equinox: the mouse aims in zero gravity the same as in the rest of the game (no more capped, stick-like turning).
- The vehicle weapon wheel also works on mounted guns (Alpine Escape's snowmobile gun and rockets).
- PC Settings opened from the pause screen: the arrows and left/right change the values again.

## v1.4.1

- Fixed a crash when a mission loads its next area (The Exchange), and the mouse in the menus.
